# Code of Conduct

WP Reset Filters follows the
[WordPress Community Code of Conduct](https://make.wordpress.org/handbook/community-code-of-conduct/).

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, respectful, collaborative, and constructive. Focus discussion
on the work, welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels,
and assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior directly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[John James Jacoby](mailto:johnjamesjacoby@me.com). Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and discreetly as practical. Good-faith reporters and participants in
an investigation will not face retaliation.
